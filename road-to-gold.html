Road to Gold

Brazil leads the Olympic beach volleyball podium

Brazil has earned 14 team medals, three more than the United States with 11. That is the most sustained success in the Olympic beach volleyball medal table, but the podium is not limited to two countries: 15 countries have won a medal.

Brazil reached the podium at every Games from 1996 through 2016, then returned to the top of the women’s podium in Paris. The United States has more golds, while Brazil’s seven silvers match the U.S. total of seven golds. More recent editions brought men’s golds for Norway in Tokyo and Sweden in Paris.

Observation count: 756 athlete-event records produce 756 points, representing 520 distinct source IDs. All 756 beach-volleyball records in the source have reported ages.

The median age is 29. A point is one person’s participation in one Games and event; an athlete who returns in later editions appears again, so the points are records, not unique people.

Methodology

  • The source is olympicAthletes::olympic_athletes, a 315,094-row, 16-column athlete-event dataset. The package describes 1896–2016 records as extending the Olympic history dataset, with later records sourced from Olympedia.
  • Beach volleyball is selected by requiring sport == "Volleyball" and a beach-volleyball match in event. The package’s 4,792-row volleyball_athletes subset contains indoor and beach volleyball; it is not used as a beach-volleyball count.
  • For 1996–2016, the source repeats the medal on both athletes’ rows. We retain one row for each Games year, event, team identifier, and medal, then normalize team suffixes (such as -1) only when aggregating results by country.
  • The package data used here has no beach-volleyball medal labels for 2020 or 2024. We add the six podium teams for each of those editions from the Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 official results pages. These are team-level rows, so no athlete-level medal duplication is introduced.
  • Participation charts count athlete-event rows; medal charts count medal-winning teams. A pair therefore contributes two participation records but only one medal.
  • To render the site, install R packages olympicAthletes, tidyverse, ggplot2, plotly, crosstalk, leaflet, rnaturalearth, and geojsonsf, then run quarto render.
