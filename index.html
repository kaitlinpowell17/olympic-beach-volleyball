NoteWhat the data can—and cannot—show

The source documentation defines each row as one athlete’s participation in a Games and event; the records have athlete IDs and do not include coaches or other staff. This chart includes Summer Volleyball records with reported age. Of 4,792 such event records from 1964–2024, 756 are beach volleyball across eight Games (84 in 1996 and 96 per edition from 2000 through 2024), and 4,027 are indoor volleyball. Nine Volleyball records have no age and are not plotted.

The beach-only subset is too small to supply thousands of distinct observations. Indoor volleyball is included as historical context for the longer Olympic timeline, while its smaller, muted markers keep beach volleyball prominent. The chart shows every age-reported Volleyball record once; a beach-specific graphic with thousands of points would need an additional rally-by-rally dataset, such as official FIVB/Volleyball World DataVolley records for Olympic beach matches.
